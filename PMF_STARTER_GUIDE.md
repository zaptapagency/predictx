## 🎤 HOW TO GET INTERVIEWS

### The Math
- Send 100 cold emails → Get 5-10 meetings (5-10% response)
- Interview 50 customers → Find clear pattern (takes 3 months)
- Get 10 beta users → Validate solution works

### Fastest Ways (Ranked by conversion)
1. **Warm intro** (ask LinkedIn connections) → 50% conversion ⭐⭐⭐
2. **Industry community** (Slack, Reddit, Discord) → 20% conversion ⭐⭐
3. **Cold email** (you write it) → 5-10% conversion ⭐
4. **Twitter/LinkedIn** (comment, start convos) → 10% conversion ⭐

### Cold Email That Works
```
Subject: [Problem mention]

Hi [Name],

I noticed you're [role] at [company].

I'm researching how [specific vertical] currently 
[solve specific problem]. Not selling anything - 
just trying to understand what's painful.

20 min?

[Your name]
[Calendar link]
```

## 🚨 RED FLAGS (Means pivot)

**After 15 interviews, if you see this:**
- ❌ Every customer describes different problem
- ❌ Problem not in top 3 priorities
- ❌ Average pain level <6/10
- ❌ No one has budget for solution
- ❌ Timeline is "next year"

**Action:** Pick different vertical, start over

**After 30 interviews, if you see this:**
- ❌ Still no clear pattern
- ❌ <50% say they'd pay
- ❌ Average price is <$50/month
- ❌ Timeline is 6+ months

**Action:** Problem isn't big enough, pivot

**After 50 interviews:**
- ❌ MVP doesn't activate users
- ❌ No one wants to pay
- ❌ Churn is >50% per week
- ❌ No referrals

**Action:** You don't have PMF yet

---

## 🟢 GREEN FLAGS (Means go)

**When you're winning:**
- ✅ 80%+ describe same problem (word-for-word)
- ✅ 60%+ say "YES I'd pay for this"
- ✅ Average price: $X/month (tight range)
- ✅ MVP activation: 70%+ complete first action
- ✅ Users ask "When can I buy?"
- ✅ People refer you without asking

**You now have:** 🎉 Product-Market Fit

## 💰 PRICING VALIDATION

**After 20 interviews, calculate price:**

```
Cost of Problem = 
  (Hours wasted/week) × 
  (50 weeks/year) × 
  ($salary/hour)

Example:
  3 hours/week × 50 weeks × $50/hour = $7,500/year cost
  Minimum price: $2,500/year = $210/month
  Maximum price: $5,000/year = $420/month
  Recommended: $3,000-4,000/year = $250-330/month

Your Pricing Target: $___/month
```
