# 🚀 90-Day Path to Product-Market Fit

**Timeline:** Now to Product-Market Fit validation  
**Success:** 50 interviews completed + clear problem identified + 10+ beta users

---

## 🎯 THE MISSION

**Find ONE customer problem so painful that people will:**
1. Drop what they're doing to talk about it
2. Cry when you describe the solution
3. BEG to pay for it
4. Tell their friends about it

---

## ⏰ 90-DAY BREAKDOWN

### DAYS 1-30: CUSTOMER DISCOVERY

#### Week 1: Prep
- [ ] Day 1: Read CUSTOMER_INTERVIEW_TEMPLATE.md
- [ ] Day 2: Pick ONE vertical to focus on
  - Options: (pick your favorite)
    - [ ] Fraud detection (finance/e-commerce)
    - [ ] Churn prediction (SaaS/telecom)
    - [ ] Demand forecasting (retail/supply chain)
    - [ ] Lead scoring (enterprise sales)
    - [ ] Customer lifetime value (subscription)
  - **Decision:** Which vertical has the biggest problem?
- [ ] Day 3: Create target customer list (50 people)
  - Use: LinkedIn, ZoomInfo, Apollo.io, Company websites
  - Look for: Title + Company + Email
  - Aim for: Mix of company sizes (SMB, mid-market, enterprise)
- [ ] Day 4-5: Write cold outreach email (test with 3 friends first)
- [ ] Day 6-7: Send 20 emails

#### Week 2-3: First 10 Interviews
- [ ] Schedule 10 calls (aim for 3-4 per week)
- [ ] Conduct interviews (30 min each)
- [ ] Fill out interview summary for each one
- [ ] After 5 interviews: Look for pattern
- [ ] After 10 interviews: Can you write problem in 1 sentence?

#### Week 4: Pattern Recognition
- [ ] Review all 10 summaries
- [ ] Extract verbatim quotes (copy/paste exact words)
- [ ] Create "Problem Statement" document
- [ ] Count: How many mentioned each pain?
- [ ] Score: Qualification score for each (40/50 average?)

**Goal by Day 30:**
- ✅ 10 customers interviewed
- ✅ Clear problem statement (1-2 sentences)
- ✅ Common theme emerged (80%+ mention same problem)
- ✅ Average pain level: 7/10+
- ✅ 3 customers interested in beta

---

### DAYS 31-60: VALIDATION

#### Week 5-6: Scale Interviews (10 more)
- [ ] Schedule 10 more interviews
- [ ] Target SAME vertical (don't spread thin)
- [ ] Aim for: Same problem from 80%+
- [ ] Track: Budget they'd pay for solution
- [ ] Track: Timeline for buying (urgency)

**Questions to focus on:**
- "What are you currently paying for [tool that solves this]?"
- "Would you pay $[price] per month?"
- "When could you start using this?"

#### Week 7: Feature Prioritization
- [ ] Sketch wireframe of solution (simple, 1-2 screens)
- [ ] Show wireframe to 5 customers
- [ ] Ask: "Would this solve your problem?"
- [ ] Track feedback (what changes would help?)
- [ ] Identify: Top 3 must-have features

#### Week 8: Build MVP Roadmap
- [ ] Write 1-page product spec (features only)
- [ ] Estimate development time (2-4 weeks)
- [ ] Get 5 commitments: "I'll test this in [timeframe]"

**Goal by Day 60:**
- ✅ 20 customers interviewed (total)
- ✅ 80%+ confirm same problem
- ✅ 60%+ say they'd pay for solution
- ✅ Know average price: $[X]/month
- ✅ Know decision timeline: [X] weeks
- ✅ 5 beta users committed

---

### DAYS 61-90: SOLUTION VALIDATION

#### Week 9-10: Build Minimum Viable Product
- [ ] Pick 3 must-have features ONLY
- [ ] Build in 2 weeks (no polish, MVP quality)
- [ ] Focus: Does it solve the #1 pain point?
- [ ] Deploy to staging (not production)

**What MVP looks like:**
```
❌ Beautiful UI
❌ Fancy animations  
❌ Mobile app
❌ Advanced features

✅ Solves ONE problem well
✅ Works with existing tools
✅ Generates result (report/prediction/insight)
✅ Can be used by target customer
```

#### Week 11: Beta Testing
- [ ] Invite 5 beta users
- [ ] Send them: Link + 5-minute video explaining problem/solution
- [ ] Collect: Feedback after 1 week
- [ ] Track: Did they use it? How many times?
- [ ] Measure: Activation rate (% who completed first action)

**Questions to ask beta users:**
- "Does this solve your problem?"
- "What would make this 10x better?"
- "Would you pay for this?"
- "Who else needs this?"

#### Week 12: Refine & Scale
- [ ] Incorporate top 3 feedback items
- [ ] Invite 10 more beta users
- [ ] Measure: Same activation metrics
- [ ] Get: Referrals to similar companies
- [ ] Ask: "Can we charge you $X/month for early access?"

**Goal by Day 90:**
- ✅ 15-20 beta users testing
- ✅ 70%+ activation rate (they used it)
- ✅ 60%+ say "Yes I'd pay"
- ✅ Know exact pricing ($[X]/month)
- ✅ Have first 3-5 paying customers
- ✅ Have referral list of 20+ similar prospects

## ⚠️ RED FLAGS (Pivot if you see these)

**After 10 interviews:**
- ❌ No clear problem pattern (customers describe different problems)
- ❌ Problem not in top 3 goals for 70%+ of customers
- ❌ Avg pain level <6 (people don't care enough)
- ❌ No one has budget for solution

**Action:** Pick different vertical, start over

**After 20 interviews:**
- ❌ Still unclear what problem to solve
- ❌ 50%+ say they wouldn't pay

- ❌ Timeline is 6+ months ("nice to have")

**Action:** Pivot to different problem or customer

- ❌ Users test but don't use (churn >50% per week)

## 💰 PRICING VALIDATION FORMULA

**After 30+ interviews, calculate:**

```
Annual Cost of Problem = 
  (Hours per week spent) × 
  (50 weeks/year) × 
  (Hourly salary OR cost of problem)

Example:
- Sales team wastes 3 hours/week on lead scoring
- 3 hours × 50 weeks × $50/hour = $7,500/year cost
- Minimum price: $2,500/year (1/3 of savings)
- Maximum price: $5,000/year (2/3 of savings)
- Recommended: $3,500-4,000/year = $300-330/month

Your Price Target: $___/month
```

## 💡 PRO TIPS

### Getting Interviews
- Warm intros convert at 50%+ (ask LinkedIn connections for referrals)
- Cold email converts at 5-10% (send 20, get 1-2 calls)
- Industry Slack communities convert at 15%+ (less competitive)
- Twitter/Reddit: Comment on relevant posts, people DM you

### During Interviews
- Record audio (always ask permission first)
- Take notes on customer's EXACT words (not your interpretation)
- Ask "Why?" three times to get to root cause
- Silence is powerful (let them fill it)
- Don't pitch - just listen

### After Interviews
- Fill out summary while fresh (same day)
- Look for patterns (do 70% say same thing?)
- Save all verbatim quotes (you'll want to use them in messaging)
- Follow up in 2 weeks (nurture relationship)

### Recruiting Beta Users
- Previous interviewees first (they'll say yes)
- LinkedIn outreach: "Loved chatting. Want to try early version?"
- Referrals: "Who else has this problem?"
- Community: Post in relevant Slack/Reddit/Discord
