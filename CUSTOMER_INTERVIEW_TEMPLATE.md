# 🎤 Customer Interview Template

**Purpose:** Discover product-market fit by understanding real customer problems, workflows, and pain points.

**Duration:** 30-45 minutes  
**Frequency:** Interview 50-100 customers before building features  
**Success:** You'll know PMF when customers BEG to pay for your solution

---

## 📋 PRE-INTERVIEW CHECKLIST

**Before you call:**
- [ ] Schedule 30 min (not 1 hour - respect their time)
- [ ] Research their company (2 min on LinkedIn/website)
- [ ] Have their title written down
- [ ] Turn off Slack/email notifications
- [ ] Record audio (ask permission first)
- [ ] Have this template open
- [ ] Pick ONE vertical to focus on (don't ask generic "software" questions)

---

## 🎬 OPENING (2 minutes)

```
"Hi [Name], thanks so much for taking 30 minutes. 

I'm building a [one-sentence problem statement].
I'm not here to pitch anything - I just want to understand 
how you currently solve [the problem] and what's painful about it.

Everything you say is super valuable, and I'll take notes but 
we're just having a conversation. Sound good?"
```

**Goal:** Get them comfortable, explain you're listening (not selling)

---

## 🕵️ BACKGROUND (3-5 minutes)

### Job & Context
1. **"Tell me about your current role and what you do day-to-day."**
   - *Listen for:* Their responsibilities, who they report to, team size
   - *Write down:* Title, team structure, what success looks like for them

2. **"How long have you been in this role?"**
   - *Listen for:* Experience level, stability in company
   - *Why ask:* Newer people may have fresh perspectives

3. **"What's the size of your company and team?"**
   - *Listen for:* Budget, decision-making speed, complexity
   - *Why ask:* SMB vs Enterprise needs are different

### Tactical Context
4. **"Walk me through what a typical day looks like for you."**
   - *Listen for:* Where [your problem] fits into their workflow
   - *Write down:* Tools they use, time spent on problem area
   - *Red flag if:* Your problem isn't mentioned (not a priority)

5. **"What are your top 3 goals this quarter/year?"**
   - *Listen for:* Strategic priorities
   - *Why ask:* Your solution should ladder up to their goals
   - *Red flag if:* Your problem isn't in top 3

---

## 🔍 PROBLEM DISCOVERY (10-15 minutes) ← MOST IMPORTANT SECTION

### Current Approach
6. **"How do you currently [solve the problem]?"**
   - *Listen for:* Existing solution, tools, process, people
   - *Write down:* Exact workflow (step by step)
   - *Dig deeper:* "Walk me through exactly how you do that..."

7. **"Who else is involved in [this process]?"**
   - *Listen for:* Decision makers, stakeholders, team members
   - *Why ask:* Understand organizational complexity
   - *Write down:* Org chart snippet

8. **"How much time do you spend on [this] each week?"**
   - *Listen for:* Scale of problem
   - *Convert to:* Annual hours × salary = $ cost
   - *Red flag if:* <2 hours/week (not painful enough)

### Pain Points (The Gold)
9. **"What's frustrating about your current approach?"** ← Ask this 3 different ways
   - *Listen for:* Specific complaints, workarounds, band-aids
   - *Write down:* EXACT words they use (quote them)

10. **"What's the cost of getting this wrong?"**
    - *Listen for:* Business impact, revenue loss, customer impact
    - *Why ask:* Understanding budget justification
    - *Convert to:* "If we could save you X, what would that be worth?"

11. **"Have you tried to solve this differently?"**
    - *Listen for:* Previous tools, failed attempts, why they stopped
    - *Why ask:* Understand what doesn't work
    - *Write down:* Competitor names, tools they tried

12. **"What would an ideal solution look like?"**
    - *Listen for:* Dream solution (often reveals true pain)
    - *Red flag if:* They don't have a clear vision
    - *Why ask:* Understand what success looks like to them

### Priority & Urgency
13. **"How urgent is this for you? (1-10)"**
    - *Listen for:* Priority level
    - *Red flag if:* <6 = not urgent enough to buy
    - *Why ask:* Urgency = willingness to pay

14. **"What would need to change for this to become urgent?"**
    - *Listen for:* Triggers that unlock budget
    - *Why ask:* Understand what needs to happen first

---

## 💰 BUYING SIGNALS (5-7 minutes)

### Budget & Decision
15. **"How much budget do you have for [solving this] this year?"**
    - *Listen for:* Budget number (even if "not sure")
    - *Why ask:* Understand pricing ceiling
    - *Red flag if:* Zero budget (not a real need)

16. **"Who makes the final decision on [this type of purchase]?"**
    - *Listen for:* Budget holder, approval layers
    - *Write down:* Decision maker name/email if possible
    - *Why ask:* Understand sales process

17. **"What's your approval process look like?"**
    - *Listen for:* How long from demo to contract?
    - *Why ask:* Understand sales cycle length
    - *Examples:* "Just my approval" vs "Need CFO sign-off"

18. **"If we built [solution], when would you want to use it?"**
    - *Listen for:* Timeline, urgency
    - *Red flag if:* "Sometime next year" (low urgency)
    - *Green flag if:* "Next month" or "ASAP"

---

## 🎯 SOLUTION TESTING (5-10 minutes)

### Reactive (if you describe your solution)
19. **"Does this solve your problem?"**
    - *Listen for:* Enthusiasm level (1-10)
    - *Red flag if:* Lukewarm response
    - *Dig deeper:* "What would make this perfect for you?"

20. **"What would you change?"**
    - *Listen for:* Feature feedback
    - *Why ask:* Understand requirements
    - *Write down:* Their exact words (not your interpretation)

### Proactive (don't describe solution, let them)
19b. **"If you could wave a magic wand, what would solve this?"**
    - *Listen for:* Ideal solution
    - *Why ask:* Unbiased feature preferences
    - *Write down:* Feature requests

20b. **"Would you pay for a solution that [describe your product]?"**
    - *Listen for:* Price sensitivity
    - *Ask directly:* "What would you pay monthly?"
    - *Red flag if:* "That's too expensive" before you said price

---

## 🚀 COMMITMENT & NEXT STEPS (2-3 minutes)

### Qualify Them
21. **"Would you be interested in trying an early version?"**
    - *Listen for:* Willingness to be beta user
    - *Red flag if:* Hesitation or excuses
    - *Green flag if:* "Yes, when can I start?"

22. **"Can I follow up in 2 weeks to see if things have changed?"**
    - *Listen for:* Openness to future conversation
    - *Why ask:* Prioritize for product roadmap

### Get Referrals
23. **"Who else in your industry faces this problem?"**
    - *Listen for:* Similar personas
    - *Why ask:* Find more customers to interview
    - *Get:* Name, email, permission to mention referrer

---

## 📝 CLOSING (1 minute)

```
"This was super helpful. I really appreciate your time.

I'm going to [specific action - build feature/iterate].
I'll follow up with you in [2 weeks] to get your feedback.

Thanks again!"
```

## 📊 POST-INTERVIEW ANALYSIS

**Immediately after call (while fresh):**

### Scoring
Rate this customer on each dimension:

| Dimension | Score (1-10) | Notes |
|-----------|--------------|-------|
| Problem Clarity | ___ | Do they have a clear problem? |
| Pain Level | ___ | How painful is it? |
| Budget | ___ | Do they have money to spend? |
| Urgency | ___ | Do they want to solve it NOW? |
| Decision Speed | ___ | How long from demo to purchase? |
| **TOTAL** | **___/50** | **>35 = Qualified, <20 = Bad fit** |

### Key Insights to Extract

1. **Verbatim Quotes** (copy their exact words)
   ```
   "This is the #1 thing killing my productivity"
   "We lose ~$50K per month when X happens"
   "We've tried 3 different tools and nothing worked"
   ```

2. **Pain Level** (1-10 scale)
   ```
   <4 = Nice to have
   5-6 = Should solve
   7-8 = Must solve
   9-10 = URGENT (will pay any price)
   ```

3. **Workflow** (exactly how they do it now)
   ```
   Step 1: Export data from system A
   Step 2: Manually transform in Excel (3 hours/week!)
   Step 3: Import into system B
   Step 4: Generate report
   ```

4. **Real Cost** (translate time to $)
   ```
   3 hours/week × 50 weeks/year × $100/hour = $15,000/year cost
   → We should charge minimum $5,000/year (1/3 of savings)
   ```

5. **Competitor/Alternative** (what they use now)
   ```
   "We use [Tool X] but it's clunky"
   "We built internal scripts but they break constantly"
   "We just do it manually because nothing fits"
   ```

6. **Green Flags** (this is a buyer)
   ```
   ✅ Problem is in top 3 goals
   ✅ Urgency is 8+
   ✅ Budget exists
   ✅ Decision maker is available
   ✅ Timeline is 1-2 months
   ```

7. **Red Flags** (waste of time)
   ```
   ❌ "Nice to have" only
   ❌ Urgency is 3-4
   ❌ "We'll look next year"
   ❌ Budget needs approval from 5 people
   ❌ Problem not in top 3 priorities
   ```

## 🎯 INTERVIEW GOALS BY PHASE

### Phase 1: Discovery (Customers 1-15)
**Goal:** Find the problem that keeps people awake at night
- [ ] Problem is clear to them
- [ ] They're spending time/money on it
- [ ] They've tried to solve it before
- [ ] Failure to solve costs them money/time

**Success:** You hear the SAME problem from 70% of customers

### Phase 2: Validation (Customers 16-35)
**Goal:** Confirm it's the RIGHT problem to solve
- [ ] 80%+ say "YES I have this problem"
- [ ] 60%+ say they'd pay for a solution
- [ ] 40%+ say they'd pay >$100/month
- [ ] Pattern emerges: They all describe similar workflow

**Success:** You can write the problem in one sentence that 100% recognize

### Phase 3: Solution Testing (Customers 36-50)
**Goal:** Validate your solution actually solves it
- [ ] Show wireframes/mockups/demo
- [ ] 80%+ say "Yes this would help"
- [ ] Get specific feature feedback
- [ ] Get commitment to beta test

**Success:** You have 10+ beta users ready to use your product

## 🚀 HOW TO GET INTERVIEWS

### Where to Find Customers

**Cold Outreach (Highest conversion)**
```
Email Template:

Subject: 20-minute customer research call? [Problem + value]

Hi [Name],

I noticed you're [role] at [company].

I'm researching how [your vertical] teams currently 
[solve the problem]. I'm not selling anything - 
just trying to understand the pain points.

Would you have 20 minutes this week for a quick call?

[Your name]
[Link to calendar]
```

**Conversion rate:** 5-10% response rate = 10-20 responses per 100 emails

### Response Rates by Approach
| Approach | Response Rate | Time Investment |
|----------|--------------|-----------------|
| Cold email | 5-10% | High |
| LinkedIn DM | 3-5% | Medium |
| Warm intro | 30-50% | Highest |
| Community (Reddit, Slack) | 10-20% | Medium |
| Conference attendees | 15-25% | Medium |
| Your network | 50%+ | Low |

**Pro tip:** Start with your warm network (50% response rate), then scale to cold outreach

## 💡 INTERVIEWER TIPS

### What Great Interviewers Do
✅ Listen more than they talk  
✅ Ask "Why?" three times to get to root cause  
✅ Take notes on exact words (not interpretations)  
✅ Make customer feel heard and valued  
✅ Follow up with referrals (best sourcing)  

### What Bad Interviewers Do
❌ Pitch their solution too early  
❌ Put words in customer's mouth  
❌ Ask yes/no questions (closed-ended)  
❌ Interrupt with their own ideas  
❌ Only talk to people like them  

### Magic Questions
```
1. "Tell me more about that" (when they mention pain)
2. "Why is that a problem?" (dig for impact)
3. "How much does that cost you?" (get to budget)
4. "What would be ideal?" (learn dream solution)
5. "Would you pay for that?" (test if they'll buy)
```

## 🎬 REAL EXAMPLE

**Bad Interview (Don't do this):**
```
You: "Do you think AI predictions are useful?"
Customer: "Yeah, I guess"
You: "Would you pay $29/month for predictions?"
Customer: "Maybe, sounds interesting"
→ Result: No learning, customer doesn't care
```

**Good Interview (Do this):**
```
You: "Walk me through how you currently forecast demand"
Customer: "We use Excel and SQL queries, takes 3 days"
You: "Why 3 days?"
Customer: "Data is scattered, we manually pull from 5 systems"
You: "What happens when you're late?"
Customer: "We've lost $200K in orders when forecast is wrong"
You: "If we could cut that to 30 minutes, what's that worth?"
Customer: "That's worth at least $5K/month to us"
→ Result: Clear problem, $60K/year budget unlocked
```
