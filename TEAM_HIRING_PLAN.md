## 🎯 HIRE FOR GROWTH (Priority order)

### Priority 1: Sales (Month 2)
**Why:** Nothing matters without customers  
**Role:** Growth/Sales Contractor  
**Criteria:**
- 5+ years SaaS sales experience
- Has sold $20-100/month products before
- Has network in [your vertical]
- Can work 10-15 hrs/week

**Compensation:**
- Base: $2,000/month
- Commission: 10% of first 12 months ACV
  - Example: Close $10K/year deal = $1,000 commission
  - Example: Close $50K/year deal = $5,000 commission

**Job Description:**
```
Title: Growth/Sales Contractor - ForecastX

You'll help us land our first 100 customers.

What you'll do:
- Outreach to 50 qualified prospects/month
- Run discovery calls
- Close 2-5 pilots/month
- Provide product feedback

What we need:
- 5+ years SaaS sales (SMB or mid-market focus)
- Your network in [Customer Success / Retention]
- 10-15 hours/week
- Commission-hungry (upside-focused)

Compensation:
- $2,000/month base
- 10% commission on first-year ACV
- Equity option: 0.1-0.5% vesting 4 years
```

**Where to find:**
- LinkedIn: Search "SaaS Sales" in your vertical
- AngelList: Post on Jobs
- Slack communities: [Your vertical] community
- Your network: Ask VCs/founders for referrals

**Interview questions:**
- "Tell me about a deal you closed. What was the size?"
- "Why did that customer buy from you?"
- "Walk me through your sales process."
- "What's your close rate on cold outreach?"
- "If I gave you $10K to acquire customers, how would you spend it?"

### Priority 2: Engineer (Month 4)

**Role:** Full-stack Engineer  
**Criteria:**

## ⚡ HIRING STRATEGY: HIRE SLOW, FIRE FAST

**Red flags (don't hire):**
- ❌ They don't understand your vertical
- ❌ They want salary but no upside bet
- ❌ They can't explain what they've sold before
- ❌ They badmouth previous employers
- ❌ They won't commit (unclear about hours/availability)

**Green flags (hire them):**
- ✅ Obsessed with solving the problem
- ✅ Has network/credibility in space
- ✅ Understands customer pain intimately
- ✅ Willing to work for commission/equity early
- ✅ Hustler mentality (no job too small)
