## 💰 THE CHURN PROBLEM (Why This Market is Gold)

### The Math (Real numbers)
```
Company: SaaS with $10M ARR
- Current monthly churn: 5% ($415K lost/month)
- If you reduce churn by 1%: Save $830K/year
- If you reduce churn by 2%: Save $1.66M/year
- If you reduce churn by 5%: Save $4.15M/year

Minimum price they'd pay: $500K/year (50% of savings)
Maximum price they'd pay: $2M/year (50-100% of savings)
```

### Why It Matters (The Pain)
- **Revenue Impact:** 1% churn = $830K loss on $10M ARR
- **Quota Risk:** Miss retention goals = VP gets fired
- **Predictability:** Can't forecast revenue if churn is unknown
- **Competitive:** SaaS company with 10% churn is worth 2x less than 5% churn

### Current Approach (What they do now)
1. **Export:** Pull data from Salesforce/billing system
2. **Manual:** Build pivot tables in Excel
3. **Guess:** "We think these customers might churn"
4. **React:** Call them when they churn (too late)
5. **Cost:** 20+ hours/month of manual work

## 🎯 THE EXACT PROBLEM TO SOLVE

### Problem Statement (Use this in interviews)
```
"We can't predict which customers are about to churn until 
they've already left. We spend 20+ hours/month manually 
tracking customer health. By the time we notice problems, 
customers are already disengaged."
```

### The Pain Points (What they'll say)
1. **"We react instead of prevent"**
   - Currently: Customer cancels → We try to save them
   - Needed: Know 60 days before → Proactive engagement

2. **"We're flying blind on retention"**
   - Have: Customer list + billing data
   - Missing: Churn risk scoring + early warnings

3. **"Our tools don't talk to each other"**
   - Salesforce has engagement data
   - Stripe has billing data
   - No integration = manual work

4. **"We lose high-value customers"**
   - Big customers churn = massive revenue hit
   - Don't know it's coming until it's gone

5. **"We don't know what causes churn"**
   - Why did they leave? Unknown
   - What could have prevented it? No idea
   - Can't learn or improve

## 🎤 CHURN-SPECIFIC INTERVIEW QUESTIONS

**Use these instead of generic questions:**

### Current Situation (5 min)
1. "How do you currently predict which customers might churn?"
   - *Listen for:* Excel, Salesforce reports, gut feel, nothing
   - *Red flag if:* "We just wait until they leave"
   - *Green flag if:* "We have a system but it's manual"

2. "Walk me through your churn prediction process step-by-step."
   - *Listen for:* Data sources, frequency, who does it, how long
   - *Write down:* Exact workflow (this is your replacement)

3. "How much time does your team spend on retention/churn work each week?"
   - *Calculate:* Hours/week × 50 weeks × $100/hour salary = annual cost
   - *Red flag if:* <3 hours/week (not painful enough)
   - *Green flag if:* 10+ hours/week = $50K+/year cost

### The Pain (5 min)
4. "What's your biggest frustration with your current churn process?"
   - *Listen for:* Too manual, reactive, missing warning signs, reactive
   - *Copy exact words:* These are your sales bullets

5. "When was the last time a high-value customer churned unexpectedly?"
   - *Listen for:* Revenue impact, could have been prevented
   - *Ask:* "How much revenue was that?"

6. "What does it cost you when a customer churns unexpectedly?"
   - *Calculate:* If avg customer is $5K/month, losing 2/month = $120K/year
   - *This is their budget:* Can justify $30-60K/year spend to prevent it

### The Gap (5 min)
7. "If you could predict churn 60 days early, what would you do?"
   - *Listen for:* Win-back campaigns, proactive support, feature requests
   - *Why ask:* Understand what solution would be used for

8. "What information would you need to have to know if a customer is about to churn?"
   - *Listen for:* Engagement data, support tickets, usage metrics, payment issues
   - *This defines your MVP:* What data to track

9. "How many customers do you lose per month?"
   - *Calculate:* If 50 customers × $5K/month = $250K/month lost
   - *Budget justification:* "Predicting even 10% would save $300K/year"

### The Buy (5 min)
10. "How much would you pay annually for a solution that predicts churn?"
    - *Listen for:* Number or hesitation
    - *If hesitation:* "What if I told you it could save you $500K/year?"

11. "If we built this, when would you want to start using it?"
    - *Green flag if:* "Next month" or "ASAP"
    - *Red flag if:* "Next year" or "sometime"

12. "Who would need to approve a purchase like this?"
    - *Listen for:* Just them, VP, CFO, board
    - *Write down:* Approval chain

13. "What's your budget for churn/retention tools this year?"
    - *Listen for:* Number or "don't know"
    - *Why ask:* Know pricing ceiling

14. "Would you be interested in trying an early version in [2 weeks]?"
    - *Green flag if:* "Yes, when can we start?"
    - *Red flag if:* "Maybe" or "not sure"
